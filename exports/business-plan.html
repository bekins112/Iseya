01 · Executive Summary

Executive Summary

Iṣéyá is a mobile-first job marketplace for Nigeria — connecting workers with employers and recruitment agents across casual, part-time, remote, freelance and full-time work. It replaces the offline, middleman-driven hiring that dominates Africa's largest informal economy with a fast, verified and Naira-native platform.

Hiring in Nigeria still runs on word of mouth, WhatsApp groups and informal middlemen who charge job seekers one to two months of wages with no accountability. Meanwhile the enabling rails — smartphones, mobile money and digital KYC — are finally ubiquitous. Iṣéyá brings this entire flow online: workers build verified profiles and apply in a few taps; employers post jobs and hire with confidence; agents run recruitment for multiple clients from a single dashboard.

The product is already built and live. It includes role-based experiences for applicants, employers and agents; identity verification with a review workflow; tiered subscriptions billed in Naira through Paystack and Flutterwave; an admin console with granular role-based access control; automated transactional email; a support and live-chat system; Facebook auto-posting for reach; and a Job-Aid programme that monetises the job-seeker side with premium career support.

02 · Company Overview

Company Overview

Iṣéyá is a job marketplace platform operated by Renowned Technology Limited, connecting workers with employers across Nigeria. The name — Yoruba for "work" — reflects the mission: to make honest work easy to find and easy to fill, for everyone from a house cleaner in Lagos to a software engineer working remotely.

Mission

To connect every Nigerian to dignified work through a platform that is trusted, transparent and built for local realities.

Vision

To become the default hiring layer for Africa's informal and semi-formal economy — the first place workers, employers and agents go to get things done.

What Iṣéyá does today

  • For job seekers: browse and apply to jobs for free, build a profile, get verified, track applications, negotiate offers, and optionally subscribe to Job-Aid for hands-on career support.
  • For employers: post jobs, access verified applicants, manage a hiring pipeline, run interviews, extend and negotiate offers, and reach beyond the platform via social auto-posting.
  • For agents: operate as recruitment middlemen the right way — managing jobs and candidates for multiple clients from one dashboard, using a credit or subscription model.

03 · The Problem

Casual work in Nigeria is stuck offline.

The largest labour market in Africa runs on informal networks, not infrastructure. That creates friction, cost and risk on every side of the transaction.

For workers

  • Jobs are found through word of mouth, WhatsApp groups and physical noticeboards — slow, narrow and unreliable.
  • Informal middlemen charge one to two months of wages as placement fees, often with no job delivered and no recourse.
  • No verifiable identity or reputation means honest workers can't prove they're trustworthy.

For employers

  • No reliable way to verify who they're hiring, especially for in-home and on-site roles.
  • Sourcing is manual and time-consuming; quality is a gamble.
  • Existing job boards are built for formal, white-collar roles and priced for enterprises — not the SMEs and households that drive casual hiring.

For the market

  • No trusted, structured, mobile-native place where supply and demand for everyday work actually meet.
  • Payments, identity and accountability are fragmented across informal channels.
The gap

The rails for digital hiring — smartphones, mobile money and KYC — are finally in place. What's missing is a trusted marketplace that ties them together for the 80% of work that is informal. That is the gap Iṣéyá fills.

07 · Technology

What's already built.

Iṣéyá is not a concept — it is a live, production platform. Capital accelerates growth on top of a working product, not a prototype.

Shipped & operating

  • Identity verification with document upload, selfie check and admin review workflow.
  • Role-based access control across applicants, employers, agents and multi-tier admins.
  • Tiered subscriptions billed in Naira via Paystack and Flutterwave, with secure payment verification.
  • Job-Aid programme — seeker plans, per-benefit request flows and admin fulfilment.
  • Facebook auto-posting to extend job reach off-platform.
  • Email automation for verification, resets, notifications and lifecycle messaging.
  • Support & live chat with an AI assistant and human-handoff to admins.
  • Admin console for users, jobs, applications, transactions, promotions and analytics.
  • Applicant tools — CV refining, recommendations, interview booking, counter-offers.
  • Secure auth — email/password with CAPTCHA, Google sign-in and hardened sessions.

Architecture

A modern, cloud-native stack: a React & TypeScript mobile-first web client, an Express (Node.js) API, and a PostgreSQL database. Payments run through Paystack and Flutterwave; transactional email through managed providers. The system is built to scale horizontally as demand grows, with security practices including hashed credentials, server-side sessions and CAPTCHA-protected auth, aligned to the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR).

09 · Go-to-Market

City-by-city, supply-side first.

Marketplaces win by solving the chicken-and-egg problem locally. Iṣéyá concentrates liquidity in one city at a time, seeding verified worker supply first so employers find immediate value.

The playbook

  1. Seed supply: recruit and verify workers in a target city through agents, community partners and referrals — free to join lowers the barrier.
  2. Activate demand: onboard SMEs and households with free posting; convert to paid tiers as they experience verified, faster hiring.
  3. Enlist agents: convert existing middlemen into platform agents who bring their own worker and employer networks.
  4. Amplify reach: Facebook auto-posting and promotions extend every listing beyond the platform.
  5. Densify, then expand: reach liquidity in one city, then replicate the motion in the next.

Channels

  • Agent-led community recruitment
  • Social & digital acquisition (incl. Facebook auto-posting)
  • Referral loops on both sides
  • SME & sector partnerships
  • Verification as an on-ramp to trust
  • Job-Aid as a seeker-side growth & monetisation engine

10 · Operations, Trust & Safety

Operations, trust & safety.

Trust is the product. Iṣéyá's operating model is designed to keep both sides of the marketplace safe, accountable and coming back.

Verification operations

A human-in-the-loop review team validates IDs and selfies before badges are granted, balancing speed with fraud prevention. Verification expires after 30 days, keeping the trust graph fresh.

Ratings & accountability

Admin-scored assessments after interviews give employers a reliable signal and give workers a way to build reputation.

Support

Ticketing plus AI-assisted live chat with human handoff resolves issues and reduces churn.

Fraud & abuse controls

Report flows for jobs and users, admin review and action, CAPTCHA-protected auth and secure payment verification with metadata and amount checks.

Data protection

NDPR-aligned handling: hashed passwords, secure server-side sessions, and controlled access to sensitive verification documents.

Agent oversight

Agents operate within platform rules and tooling, replacing opaque, unaccountable middlemen with a transparent system.
